How it works

Three steps, no sign-up

From an empty canvas to a board your whole team is working on, in about a minute.

  1. 1

    Create a board

    Start blank or from a template. Drop in notes, pictures, links and lists, and put them wherever they make sense to you.

  2. 2

    Share the invite link

    Send the link however you like. Teammates open it in a browser or the desktop app and land right on the board.

  3. 3

    Edit together, live

    See each other's cursors and changes as they happen, even when two people type in the same note.

Features

Everything a board needs

Collect anything, arrange it freely, and add structure when you are ready for it.

  • Notes

    Rich text with headings, lists and checklists. Colour a note to make it stand out.

  • Images with crop

    Drop in pictures, add captions, then crop, rotate or flip them without leaving the board.

  • Links and video

    Paste a link for a tidy preview. Videos and music from popular sites play right on the board.

  • To-do lists

    Tick tasks off and watch the progress bar fill. Set a reminder for the ones with a deadline.

  • Columns

    Stack cards in columns for stages, days or a simple kanban, and drag them between columns.

  • Boards inside boards

    Put a board on a board to keep big projects clear. Breadcrumbs always show the way back.

  • Lines and arrows

    Connect cards to show order, cause or flow, and label the connection.

  • Comments

    Talk it through in a thread next to the work, and mention a teammate to get their eyes on it.

  • Sketches

    Draw freehand with a mouse, a pen or a finger when words are not the quickest way.

  • Templates

    Project plan, weekly planner, brainstorm, moodboard, meeting notes, retrospective and storyboard.

  • Works offline

    Every board is saved on your device. Keep going without a connection and sync when you are back.

  • Desktop apps

    Install it on Windows, macOS or Linux, or use the web app in any modern browser.

Free, really

Not a trial. Not a starter plan.

There is nothing to upgrade to. Every feature is there for everyone, from the first board.

  • No accounts. Pick a name and a colour so your teammates know who is who. That is the whole setup.
  • No ads. Nothing to sell you, and nobody to sell your attention to.
  • No limits. As many boards, cards and teammates as you like.
  • Open source. MIT licensed. Read the code, change it, or run your own copy.

So how can it be free?

Most board apps keep your work on their servers. Servers cost money every month, so you pay every month.

NoemaBoard turns that around. Each board lives on the devices of the people who use it, and those devices sync with each other directly. A small sync service keeps an encrypted copy so shared boards are always within reach, and it runs on a free hosting plan.

No big server bill means nothing to pass on to you.

If the free plan's daily allowance is ever used up, the sync service pauses until the next day. Boards keep working, and devices keep syncing directly.

Privacy

Your boards are yours to read

Not ours, and not a server's.

  • End-to-end encrypted

    Boards are encrypted on your device before they travel, and only people with the board's key can open them.

  • The server can't read them

    The sync service stores and passes on encrypted data it has no key for. It can't see your notes, pictures or titles.

  • No tracking

    No analytics, no ads, no cookies, in the app and on this page.

FAQ

Good questions

Is it really free?

Yes. There is no paid plan, no trial period and no ads. Boards live on your own devices, and the always-on sync service runs on a free hosting plan, so there is no running cost to recover from you.

Do I need an account?

No. Open the app, choose the name and colour your teammates will see, and start. To work with someone, send them the board's invite link.

Is my data safe?

Your boards are saved on your device and are end-to-end encrypted whenever they sync, so the sync service only holds data it can't read. Deleted cards wait in the trash for 90 days, and you can save a full backup at any time. One thing to keep in mind: anyone with a board's invite link can open and edit it, so share links only with people you trust.

What if my teammates are offline?

Just keep working. Your changes are saved on your device and reach the always-on sync service as soon as you are online, so teammates get them the next time they open the board, even if you are offline by then.

Does it work on phones?

Yes, in your phone's browser. Pan with one finger, pinch to zoom and double-tap to add a note. Big boards are most comfortable on a larger screen, and there are no separate phone apps yet.

Can I host it myself?

Yes. NoemaBoard is open source under the MIT license. You can publish your own copy of the web app together with its sync service on a free hosting plan, or run the sync server on any computer you trust.
