# Launch posts (drafts)

Ready-to-paste drafts. Edit them freely: they read best in your own voice.

Before posting:
- Link to the **front page**: https://robertrigopoulos.github.io/noemaboard/ (LinkedIn shows a preview for this address; the Cloudflare address https://app.noemaboard.workers.dev shows the same page)
- **Never post a board invite link.** Anyone with an invite link can edit that board.
- **Attach a short screen recording** (30–60 seconds) of two windows editing the same board at
  once. On Windows: *Snipping Tool → Record*. Video gets far more reach than a plain link.
- Once the code is on GitHub, add the repository link: "open source" is a big trust signal.

## LinkedIn

> I got tired of paying monthly for a place to put my ideas, so I built one that's free.
>
> **NoemaBoard** is a visual board for collecting and organising ideas: notes, images, links,
> to-do lists, columns and boards inside boards, all on an endless canvas. Your team works on it
> together, live. You see each other's cursors and edits as they happen.
>
> What makes it different:
> - **Free for everyone.** No accounts, no subscription, no "upgrade to add more cards". There
>   are no limits.
> - **Your boards stay with you.** Boards live on your own devices and sync directly between
>   teammates, end-to-end encrypted.
> - **Share with a link.** Send it to your team and they can join from any browser, no sign-up.
> - **Works offline**, with desktop apps for Windows, Mac and Linux.
> - **Open source**, so anyone can check how it works or host their own.
>
> It's named after *noema*, the Greek word for "a thought", because that's what a board is:
> a place to lay your thoughts out next to each other.
>
> Try it here: https://robertrigopoulos.github.io/noemaboard/
>
> It's early, and I'd love feedback from anyone who plans projects, makes moodboards, or
> brainstorms with a team. What would you use it for?
>
> #productivity #opensource #design #collaboration #creativity
